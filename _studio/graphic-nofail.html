Why UST died · 01
Backing was reflexive
The collateral was the token the protocol printed. sUSD is backed by USDG, an asset the protocol cannot mint.
Why UST died · 02
Printing was unbounded
346M LUNA became 6.5T in six days. STYX is only ever minted for the (1−CR) slice, and that slice shrinks as CR rises under stress.
Why UST died · 03
No floor existed
Nothing stopped the loop. CR cannot fall below 55%, so every sUSD always has hard USDG behind it.
